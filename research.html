Research

Ocean–Atmosphere CO₂ Dynamics

SOCAT-based pCO₂ climatologies, Observing System Simulation Experiments (OSSEs), and subgrid-scale uncertainty quantification across the global ocean.

Graph Reservoir Computing for Climate Forecasting

Development of a Graph RC architecture for spatiotemporal drought prediction using CONUS404 data, hybrid ML–physics modeling, and node-level forecasting.

Teleconnections and Large-Scale Predictors

SST anomaly correlation maps, empirical teleconnection identification, and spatial subsampling using optimization and simulated annealing.
